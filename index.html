About

Emily's BnB is a tiny fostering program in Milwaukee, Wisconsin specializing in undersocialized small cats and kittens. We act as a middle-ground for the Wisconsin Humane Society, allowing these animals precious space to destress, get used to being cared for in a home environment, and learn to trust humans before transitioning to their adopter's home!

Emily's BnB takes on individual and single-litter cases to give each kitten the best of our undivided attention. We exclusively use positive reinforcement with our guests and do not believe in socialization-by-force.

Please note: Emily's BnB will never ask for payment or billing information. All adoptions are handled exclusively through the Wisconsin Humane Society's Foster Facilitated Adoption service. For more information, please visit their website.

History

Emily's BnB is dedicated to the memory of Miss Emily Montgomery.
Miss Emily was born on 17 May 2023. She was a highly intelligent, loving companion and fierce hunter of shoestrings (AKA 'spaghetti'). She suddenly passed on 25 July 2026 from large cell renal lymphoma.
